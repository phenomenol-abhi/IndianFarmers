Our Beginning

A Story Born in India's Heartland

The Indian Farmers was founded with a simple yet powerful vision — to bring the purest, most nutritious wheat flour from India's fertile farmlands directly to every household.

Started by Shubham Agro Product in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, we began our journey from the wheat-rich plains of the Ganga-Yamuna Doab. What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted brand serving thousands of families across the region.

Our founder believed that every family deserves access to genuinely pure, chemical-free flour — the kind our grandparents grew up with. That belief drives everything we do today.

Our Mission

Empowering Indian Farmers

Wheat Sourcing

Sourced from India's Golden Belt

Our wheat is sourced from the fertile plains of the Ganga-Yamuna Doab in Uttar Pradesh — one of India's most prolific wheat-growing regions known for its nutrient-rich soil and ideal climate.

Direct Farm Partnerships

We partner directly with farmer cooperatives across Prayagraj, Varanasi, and surrounding districts.

Selective Grain Picking

Only the finest, plumpest wheat grains are selected. We reject up to 15% of the harvest to ensure top quality.

Farm-to-Mill in 24 Hours

Freshly harvested wheat reaches our mill within 24 hours to lock in freshness and nutrients.

Our Process

Traditional Stone-Grinding Process

We preserve the ancient art of chakki grinding, ensuring every grain retains its natural nutrition and authentic taste.

Step 01

Grain Selection & Cleaning

Fresh wheat grains are hand-selected from our partner farms and thoroughly cleaned. We use natural winnowing to separate impurities, just like traditional methods.

1
2
Step 02

Traditional Chakki Grinding

Our stone chakki (grinding mill) operates at low speeds to prevent heat buildup, preserving all natural nutrients, fiber, and the authentic wheat flavor that modern roller mills destroy.

Step 03

Quality Testing & Assurance

Every batch is rigorously tested in our lab using fixed SOP standards. We check for purity, moisture content, fiber levels, and nutritional value — ensuring only the best reaches you.

3
4
Step 04

Hygienic Packing & Dispatch

The flour is packed in airtight, food-grade packaging with tamper-proof seals in a hygienic, GMP-certified facility. From our mill to your doorstep — untouched quality guaranteed.
